# Loom 04 — Dense 100+ agents

- **Asset:** `04-dense-100-agents.png`
- **Lifecycle:** `current`
- **Boundary:** `CONCEPT / SYNTHETIC DATA`

## User job

Navigate a large execution with more than 100 unique agents without receiving one microscopic lane or list row per agent. Overview scale groups work into deterministic task/workstream bundles; semantic zoom progressively reveals agent groups, individual agents, branches, and events.

## Production authorities

- Agents owns deduplicated root/subagent identities and parentage; Work owns task/workstream identities and overlapping participation records; Sessions/LCM owns loaded-page coverage and exact transcripts.
- Admitted hook/events own individual execution facts, local Git/Code owns diff/commit/test evidence, and Delivery owns repository, PR, and outcome cross-links.
- A deterministic worker/layout projection owns stable clustering, density aggregation, and semantic-zoom coordinates without changing evidence classes. Provider state remains read-only and private reasoning unavailable.

## Count semantics

The header's agent total is the deduplicated count of **unique root agents plus subagents** within the loaded page and active filters. Workstream badges show **participations**, not unique agents. One agent may participate in multiple workstreams, so workstream participation totals may exceed the header's unique-agent total. Every count exposes its scope and definition; no apparently conflicting unlabeled totals are allowed.

## Interaction model

- Bundle by workstream, task, repository, or outcome; expand around the selected bundle while unrelated work compresses.
- Search/filter by agent, task, file, event type, evidence state, unresolved-only, or high-risk-only.
- A virtualized branch navigator supports keyboard traversal, pinned branches, path-to-root, and path-to-outcome.
- Edge bundling, lazy evidence loading, stable layout, and density aggregation keep the main story legible.

## Evidence and honesty

Aggregation never upgrades correlation. Bundle summaries report the mix of `exact`, `explicit`, `inferred`, `ambiguous`, `stale`, and `unavailable` evidence. Exact agent tree, event table, and transcript fallbacks remain available, including duplicate workstream participations and their unique identities.

## Scale and accessibility acceptance

Dense-real-data tests cover representative 100+, 500+, and long-history cases for stable positions, virtualized navigation, and bounded rendering. Keyboard, reduced motion, exact tree/table/transcript fallback, visible focus, and 200% zoom/reflow are required. The concept/synthetic boundary remains visible.
