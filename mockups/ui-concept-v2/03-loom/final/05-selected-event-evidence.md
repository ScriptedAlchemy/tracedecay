# Loom 05 — Selected event evidence

- **Asset:** `05-selected-event-evidence.png`
- **Lifecycle:** `current`
- **Boundary:** `CONCEPT / SYNTHETIC DATA`

## User job

Inspect what happened at one selected event without squeezing exact evidence into a narrow sidebar. The selected-event workspace gives a roomy, resizable center to the exact hook/message/tool event, transcript excerpt, task, diff/hunk, causal neighborhood, and source identity.

## Production authorities

- The admitted hook/event record owns the selected event; Sessions/LCM owns loaded-page position, session identity, and exact transcript excerpts; Agents owns attribution and parentage.
- Work owns the associated task, local Git/Code owns worktree, branch, commit, symbol, diff/hunk, and test evidence, and Delivery owns PR/review/outcome cross-links.
- The deterministic layout/scene projection owns only selection geometry and causal-neighborhood presentation. Exact DOM evidence remains authoritative; provider state is read-only and private reasoning unavailable.

## Entry and visible state

Selecting an event pauses replay and enters an adjustable split or dedicated workspace. A breadcrumb and persistent time minimap provide `Back to Loom` without losing the cursor. Evidence modes group content as Story, Code & Impact, Evidence, and Feedback rather than a long strip of tiny tabs.

## Interaction model

- Selecting a persisted decision highlights the events, files, symbols, tests, or task transitions it produced when the link is evidenced.
- Selecting a code/hook event pivots back to its source transcript, task, agent, worktree, branch, commit, or Delivery episode.
- Panes are resizable/collapsible and can promote the exact transcript or diff to focus mode.
- Cross-page links to Sessions, Agents, Work, Code, and Delivery preserve the selected event and loaded-page timestamp.

## Evidence and honesty

Exact source text is never replaced by a narrative summary. Every relation uses the canonical evidence class. Persisted reasoning summaries and explicit decisions may be shown; private chain-of-thought remains unavailable. Ambiguous identity, stale joins, and missing sources remain visible beside the evidence they limit.

## Scale and accessibility acceptance

Long transcripts and diffs virtualize without losing find, line anchors, or selection. Keyboard pane navigation, reduced motion, exact table/tree/transcript/diff fallbacks, 200% zoom/reflow, and dense-real-data behavior are required. `CONCEPT / SYNTHETIC DATA` remains visible.
