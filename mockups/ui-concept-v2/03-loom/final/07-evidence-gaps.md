# Loom 07 — Evidence gaps

- **Asset:** `07-evidence-gaps.png`
- **Lifecycle:** `current`
- **Boundary:** `CONCEPT / SYNTHETIC DATA`

## User job

Recognize what Loom cannot prove: missing transcript pages, unavailable private reasoning, ambiguous agent attribution, stale provider data, incomplete pagination, or code/work that has no matching episode. Gaps are first-class review states, not empty success or decorative fog.

## Production authorities

- Sessions/LCM owns loaded-page and transcript coverage; admitted hook/event records own observed execution; Agents owns known parentage; Work owns task identity; local Git/Code owns diff, commit, and test evidence; Delivery owns PR/outcome cross-links.
- The local adjudication store owns append-only resolutions and provenance without changing immutable source facts or fabricating attribution.
- The deterministic layout/scene projection renders source gaps and candidate relations as typed states only. Provider data remains read-only, and missing or private reasoning remains explicitly unavailable.

## Entry and visible state

Evidence filters or a selected gap focus the affected time range and causal neighborhood. The view identifies the missing source, what claims it limits, whether a refresh/re-ingest is possible, and which downstream relations remain exact despite the gap.

## Local adjudication

`Record resolution` appends a local adjudication with author, time, rationale, and source references. It never rewrites immutable events, silently changes attribution, fills a transcript, or converts an inference into fact. Later evidence may supersede the adjudication through another append-only record while preserving history.

## Evidence and honesty

The canonical ladder—`exact`, `explicit`, `inferred`, `ambiguous`, `stale`, and `unavailable`—is visible in both the weave and exact fallbacks. Missing/private reasoning is explicitly unavailable. Ambiguous candidates remain candidates; stale evidence retains its source timestamp; no inferred identity or event is fabricated.

## Interaction model

- Focus the gap's path-to-root and path-to-outcome while compressing unaffected branches.
- Inspect candidate identities or sources without forcing resolution.
- Retry an authorized local refresh/re-ingest only through a real production path; otherwise show unavailable/not configured.
- Open the exact gap ledger, transcript coverage table, or branch tree from the same selection.

## Scale and accessibility acceptance

Keyboard navigation, reduced motion, sufficient gap/focus contrast, exact table/tree/transcript fallbacks, dense-real-data aggregation, and 200% zoom/reflow are mandatory. The visible `CONCEPT / SYNTHETIC DATA` boundary remains.
