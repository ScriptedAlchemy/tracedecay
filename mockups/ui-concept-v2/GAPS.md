# Product concept and production gaps

This ledger separates accepted product intent from the production evidence still
needed to deliver it. The final briefs remain the product authority. The
[imported concept application](app/README.md)
and its local lookbook are useful demonstrations, not production receipts. A
truthful unavailable or partial state is valid UI; it is not evidence that the
underlying authority is connected.

## Accepted decisions now reconciled

- Brain admits registered, indexed TraceDecay projects only. Delivery admits
  pull requests for registered repositories when their tracked heads are joined
  to indexed evidence. Provider discovery or a broad GitHub audit cannot admit
  arbitrary repositories or pull requests.
- Loom keeps follow-loaded-tail distinct from replay; replay hides unrevealed
  future events. Semantic zoom moves from group or outcome to agent, episode,
  and event, with exact evidence using the available workspace.
- The structural atlas/treemap remains a Brain and Code view option. Stable
  geometry and separate structure, dependency, change, cycle, forwarding,
  churn, exact-blob duplicate, and coverage layers complement neural and
  semantic views; they do not form a cleanliness score.
- Work proximity uses the execution weave for a many-thread overview and a
  selected encounter for pair drilldown. Candidate proximity, content conflict,
  shared work, delegation, and causality remain separate claims.
- Activity, attention, CI, proximity, health, freshness, and coverage stay
  distinct, named signals with source evidence.
