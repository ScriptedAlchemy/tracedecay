# Honest partial or unknown journey

## User job

Review a delivery even when its provenance is incomplete without mistaking missing evidence for an empty or successful result.

## Product behavior

- Missing transcript, unavailable private reasoning, ambiguous attribution, stale provider data, unmatched code, and inferred causality use separate shapes and labels.
- The journey leaves literal gaps rather than bridging them decoratively.
- Reviewers can filter unknown or weakly evidenced areas and open exact available sources.
- Transcript, event table, branch tree, diff, and provider-state fallbacks remain accessible.

## Truth boundary

The product never invents private chain-of-thought, transcript content, authorship, chronology, or causal certainty. A persisted summary is labeled as a summary, not as hidden reasoning.

## Access gates

- Keyboard navigation reaches every gap, typed-state explanation, available source, filter, and provider-state fallback.
- Reduced motion preserves missing, inferred, ambiguous, stale, and unavailable distinctions with static shape, pattern, and text.
- At 200% zoom, gaps and their explanations reflow together; no missing-state qualifier becomes clipped, hover-only, or detached from its source.
- Exact transcript, event-table, branch-tree, diff, and provider-state fallbacks expose all available evidence and literal gaps.

## Production authorities

- Each underlying authority—Work, Sessions, Agents, local Git, Code, pull requests, reviews, CI, releases, and generation freshness—reports its own ready, served-empty, partial, stale, denied, rate-limited, not-published, unavailable, or failed state.
- Missing provider authority is represented as `not_published · requires github_read_authority`; it does not erase locally available repository and pipeline evidence.
- The journey projection carries source identity and evidence grade (`exact`, `explicit`, `inferred`, `ambiguous`, or `unavailable`) without converting missing data into a successful empty result.
