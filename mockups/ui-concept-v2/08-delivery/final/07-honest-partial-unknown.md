# Honest partial or unknown journey

## User job

Review a delivery even when its provenance is incomplete without mistaking missing evidence for an empty or successful result.

## Product behavior

- Missing transcript, unavailable private reasoning, ambiguous attribution, stale provider data, unmatched code, and inferred causality use separate shapes and labels.
- The journey leaves literal gaps rather than bridging them decoratively.
- Reviewers can filter unknown or weakly evidenced areas and open exact available sources.
- Transcript, event table, branch tree, diff, and provider-state fallbacks remain accessible.

## Truth boundary

The product never invents private chain-of-thought, transcript content, authorship, chronology, or causal certainty. A persisted summary is labeled as a summary, not as hidden reasoning.
